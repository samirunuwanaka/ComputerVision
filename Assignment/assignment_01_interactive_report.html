Q7: 1D Separable Sobel Kernel

2D convolution decomposed into sequential 1D vector multiplications:

K_2D = [1, 2, 1]^T * [1, 0, -1]

Reduces complexity from $O(M \cdot N \cdot k^2)$ to $O(M \cdot N \cdot 2k)$.
